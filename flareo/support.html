About

Food Recall AU is an unofficial app that surfaces Australian food recalls sourced from the Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ) recall feed. It is built for everyday consumers, parents and people with allergies to quickly see what has been recalled, why, where, and what to do.

It is not an official FSANZ app. Always confirm details on the official recall notice before acting.

Using the app

Browse recalls

The home screen lists current recalls newest-first. Each card shows the severity, product, brand, the hazard, the affected states, and the date.

Filter & search

Use Filter to narrow by severity, state, and allergen. Tap the search button to look up a product, brand, or hazard, or browse by hazard category.

Open a recall

Tap any recall to see the full details — the hazard, where it was sold, batch/date marking, why it was recalled, what you should do, and a link to the official FSANZ recall notice.

Widgets

Add the Home Screen or Lock Screen widget to see the latest recall at a glance. Widgets refresh automatically and update when you open the app.

Severity labels

FSANZ does not publish a severity rating, so the app assigns one from the hazard text to help you triage at a glance. These labels are produced by this app, not by FSANZ:

Urgent — microbial or contamination hazards (e.g. Listeria, Salmonella, E. coli, foreign matter).
Allergen — an undeclared allergen on the label.
Advisory — lower-risk, consumer-level recalls.

Because the label is inferred from wording in the recall notice, always read the official notice for the authoritative hazard description.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the recall data come from?

Directly from the official FSANZ food-recalls RSS feed. The app fetches it automatically; there is no manual data entry.

Who decides the Urgent / Allergen / Advisory labels?

The app does, by matching keywords in the hazard text — not FSANZ. Use them as a quick guide only and rely on the official notice for the definitive hazard.

Is this the official FSANZ app?

No. Food Recall AU is an independent, unofficial app. FSANZ does not endorse it.

A recall is missing or looks out of date.

The app shows what is currently in the FSANZ feed, which can lag or change. Pull to refresh, and check the official recalls page for the most current information.

My widget isn't updating.

Open the app once so it can refresh, and ensure the device has a network connection. Widgets update on the system schedule and whenever the app fetches new data; removing and re-adding the widget also forces a refresh.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking. See Privacy below.

Privacy

Food Recall AU does not collect, store, or share personal information. There are no accounts and no third-party analytics or advertising. The app only makes a network request to download the public FSANZ recall feed and product images. Your filter and search choices stay on your device.
